# Second Order design

## Concept

A strategy room where decisions are treated like weather systems rather than spreadsheet certainties. The tension is editorial clarity against probabilistic instability.

## Signature

One lever changes a causal model, then ten thousand futures arrive as a moving probability front. Selecting an outcome turns the map into a causal lens: only its relevant paths stay strong, and an evidence strip explains why it moved. The user can compare the baseline and intervention without pretending either is a forecast.

## System

- Strategy: pure white field, oxblood decision paths, deep violet uncertainty, carbon typography.
- Palette: `oklch(1 0 0)` white, `oklch(0.46 0.17 27)` oxblood, `oklch(0.40 0.15 270)` uncertainty violet, `oklch(0.16 0.01 270)` carbon, `oklch(0.88 0.08 86)` forecast yellow.
- Type: Newsreader for strategic statements; Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono for the model.
- Layout: a wide causal map with a vertical decision rail, instant scenario positions, an inline causal explanation, and a probability weather strip. The result is one connected artifact, not a card grid.
- Shape: square paper-like panels, circular causal nodes, crisp rules.
- Motion personality: premium and deliberate. `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`, 120/240/600ms.

## Self-critique

The obvious treatment would be a finance dashboard. The causal-map structure makes relationships primary and preserves uncertainty instead of collapsing the result into one impressive-looking number. Presets teach the model by example, while shareable URL state turns a solo toy into a discussion artifact.
